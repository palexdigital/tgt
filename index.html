MÉS QUE UN CLUB

Founded in 1899 by Joan Gamper. Over 125 years of sporting excellence, values, and community.

FC Barcelona is owned by its more than 140,000 members (Socis). Through La Masia, the Barça Foundation, and the landmark Espai Barça transformation of Spotify Camp Nou, the Club continues to lead world football on and off the pitch.
